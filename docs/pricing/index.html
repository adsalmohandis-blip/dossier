Free is not a trial, and it does not expire

The tool is MIT licensed. That is not a promise we are making, it is a licence we have already granted, and it cannot be taken back. If we disappear, change our minds, or get bought, the version you have keeps working and you keep the right to use, modify and share it. We could not put a meter on it if we wanted to.

So there is no countdown. You can build Dossier into a release process that outlives the people who set it up, which is the only way a compliance tool is worth adopting at all.

What we will charge for is the work around it: preparing a release with you, and later, signing and hosting receipts so a third party can check them at a link that stays up. If that hosted tier ever ships, anyone who has recorded a release with Dossier before 20 November 2027 gets its first year free.

How payment works

The tool. Nothing to pay, no account, no sign-up. Install it from npm and it runs on your machine.

A release prepared with you. A short call first, to see whether your repository and your toolchain make this worth doing at all. Five teams get this at no charge, in exchange for telling us plainly what was useful and what was not. After that it is a fixed price agreed in writing before any work starts: half on start, half when the bundle, the record and the receipt are in your hands. Paid by bank transfer or by a card link on the invoice. If the gates cannot be wired to your build, we say so on the call and there is nothing to pay.

Hosted and signed. When it ships it will be billed monthly through a merchant of record, so your invoice and your VAT are handled properly. Until then there is no checkout, because there is nothing to check out.

Whatever happens to a subscription, receipts that have already been issued keep verifying and the bundles stay yours. Your compliance is not a hostage.

We do not watch you use it

Dossier has no telemetry and no account, so we cannot see how many people run it or what they run it on. That is deliberate: a tool that reads your gate results has no business reporting them to anyone, least of all to us.

If you want to help us work out what is worth building, run dossier report. It writes one file of counts — which gates you turned on, how many passed, which toolchain, how many decisions — prints every line of it to your terminal first, and sends nothing. Read it, and mail it to us only if you are happy with what it says. The tool behaves identically whether you do or not.

How to reach us

One address, read by a person. Not a chatbot, not a form that goes nowhere.

If you want one of the five free places, send these four things and you get a reply within two working days:

  • What the product is, and whether it carries a CE mark today
  • The toolchain: Zephyr, ESP-IDF, PlatformIO, bare CMake, something else
  • Which reports your build already produces — static analysis, coverage, SBOM
  • When your next release is, and who will assess it

If you found a bug, open an issue on GitHub instead. It gets seen faster and the fix is public.

If you want to be told when the hosted tier ships, say so in one line. One message when there is something to say, and nothing else, ever.

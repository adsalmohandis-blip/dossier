Your firmware is now a product under strict liability.

An AI agent writes part of your firmware. From 9 December 2026 that firmware is a product under EU law, and “the defect appeared after sale” is not a defence when the cause is software. Dossier records what the agent wrote, which gates it passed and who approved it, then produces a file an assessor can verify.

How it works Price Read the source

What it records

  • Every change and the model that produced it
  • Gate results from the tools you already run
  • Approvals, by name, with a date
  • Decisions with context, consequences and what they replace
  • A chained log where editing one entry breaks the next

What it refuses to do

  • Write code. Your agent does that.
  • Analyse code. cppcheck does that; Dossier reads the report.
  • Build an SBOM. Zephyr emits SPDX; Dossier links it.
  • Certify anything. A notified body does that.
  • Reach the network on its own. Nothing is uploaded unless you ask for it by name.

A gate whose report is missing is recorded as skipped, never as passed. Silence is not evidence.

Three things come out, and none of them is a certificate

Dossier does not certify, assess or approve anything, and the words on each artifact say so.
